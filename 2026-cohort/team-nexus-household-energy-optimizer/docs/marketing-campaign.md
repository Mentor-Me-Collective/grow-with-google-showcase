---

## Value proposition

A web app helping apartment renters track, compare, and lower electricity bills using simple utility bill and appliance data, with no expensive smart hardware and no tedious manual logging.

---

## Goals

Both goals run over a 90-day window from campaign launch.

| Channel | Goal |
|---|---|
| Email | 250 new web app sign-ups, 25% average open rate, 5% click-through rate across an educational nurture sequence |
| Social | 35% increase in engagement and 300 qualified web app visits (roughly 100 per month) via short-form video and carousel content on Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn |

**Tracked KPIs:** average open rate, click-through rate, engagement, conversion rate.

## Next steps

Strategy and lead creative are complete. Taking the campaign live is post-submission work (see the post-submission row of the timeline in the [README](../README.md)) and involves:

- **Expanding the nurture sequence.** The email goal assumes a multi-touch educational series; the launch email is the first of these.
- **Standing up measurement.** All four KPIs (open rate, CTR, engagement, conversion) need a sending platform and analytics connected before the 90-day window starts.
- **Finalising launch copy.** Savings figures quoted in subject lines should be tied back to what the app actually estimates, which is a comparison against EIA.gov benchmarks from user-entered usage rather than a guaranteed reduction. Any promotional offer needs to match the fact that the app is free to use.
