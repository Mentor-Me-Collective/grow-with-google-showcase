For Donors

The Food Business Journey

End your food wastage footprint in five simple, structured milestones.

1

Create an Account

Register your bakery, market, or restaurant and complete verification details to build a trustworthy profile on our network.

2

Post Surplus Food

List details including food type (bread, fruit, dairy, groceries), precise quantities, expiration deadlines, and simple collection directions.

3

Wait for a Claim

Your listing goes live instantly. Registered nearby community organizations will receive alerts about the fresh surplus food.

4

Confirm the Claim

Coordinate details with the reserving recipient through in-app channels. Keep track of scheduled slots easily.

5

Complete Pickup

Hand over the food at the agreed pickup window. The recipient delivers it to the community, reducing landfill waste footprint.

For Recipients

The Community Organization Journey

Acquire fresh, nutritious supplies directly from local food businesses nearby.

1

Create an Account

Sign up as a verified non-profit shelter, pantry, or support organization to access the active food redistribution database.

2

Discover Available Food

Check the live food directory for surplus goods. Food availability changes in real-time as local businesses list surplus.

3

Search and Filter

Refine options by distance, food category (prepared dishes, bakery items, dairy, fruits, dry goods), and expiration times.

4

View Details

Review the storage instructions, allergens, pickup address, and specific donor collection requirements before committing.

5

Claim & Coordinate

Claim the listing. Coordinate the pickup timing with the food business to collect it before expiration deadlines.
