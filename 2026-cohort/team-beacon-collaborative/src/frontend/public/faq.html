Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about sharing and finding surplus food through Nourish & Share.

Eligible food businesses, including grocery stores, restaurants, bakeries, farms, and other food providers, can register to share safe surplus food.

For the initial platform scope, recipient organisations include food pantries. Future versions can support shelters, community organisations, school meal programs, and regional food banks.

Food providers can list safe surplus with useful details such as category, quantity, expiry date, pickup window, images, and handling instructions.

After a recipient reserves available food, the donor and recipient coordinate a pickup schedule. The platform supports confirmation and pickup-status updates.

The public website explains the platform and its communities. Verified recipients access available food and the operational details needed to reserve it.

Yes. Reservation and pickup coordination are operational features for verified recipient organisations.

Donors provide expiry information when creating a listing. This helps recipients identify food that needs a timely response and coordinate pickup appropriately.

Yes. The platform’s future expansion supports restaurants, bakeries, farms, supermarkets, food manufacturers, and other food providers.

The initial MVP focuses on six selected Chicago community areas. Public information can show communities and a map while protecting sensitive operational donation details.

The initial scope is Chicago, with an architecture designed to support future expansion to additional communities, cities, and regions.
