Donation Guidelines

To ensure your donation is accepted and gets to those who need it most, please follow these guidelines.

  • Food must be safe and fit for consumption
  • Check expiration dates
  • Properly package all items
  • Include accurate quantity
  • Provide clear pickup instructions
