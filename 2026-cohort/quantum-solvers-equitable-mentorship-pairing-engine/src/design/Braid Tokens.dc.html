The mark

Four rounded bars in strict alternating over-under. The top horizontal passes over the right vertical and under the left. The bottom horizontal passes over the left and under the right. That alternation is the mark; without it this is a lattice.

Full colour left. Single colour right, used below 32px and anywhere the four colours would compete. Gaps are cut into the passing-under bar so the weave survives at small sizes.

The strand colours appear in the mark, the match reveal, and participant identity chips. Nowhere else.

Marigold takes ink

Marigold never carries white text. Fuchsia on paper is fuchsia-800; fuchsia-600 is for fills with white text.

One accent per screen

If a page has a primary action, nothing else on it is fuchsia. Health signals use ink weight and plain words, never red, amber, green.
