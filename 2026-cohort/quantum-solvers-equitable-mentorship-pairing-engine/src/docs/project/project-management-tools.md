## Risk register

Twelve risks tracked across the project, with owners and responses.

**BRAID (EQUITABLE MENTORSHIP PAIRING ENGINE) RISK REGISTER**

| Risk ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Mitigation / Response | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Poor quality or incomplete mentor/mentee profile data may result in unsuitable matches | Medium | High | High | Define clear profile requirements and validate required information before matching | Eulleria | Open |
| R02 | Matching algorithm may produce inaccurate or unsuitable recommendations | Medium | High | High | Test the matching logic with sample profiles and refine criteria based on results | Eulleria / Omogbolaga | Open |
| R03 | Insufficient number of mentors compared with mentees may leave some mentees unmatched | Medium | High | High | Monitor mentor/mentee ratios and provide an unmatched-user handling process | Benardette | Open |
| R04 | User privacy or personal data may not be adequately protected | Medium | High | High | Apply access controls, minimize unnecessary data collection and follow basic data-protection practices | Jeff | Open |
| R05 | Unauthorized access to user or administrative information | Low | High | Medium | Use authentication, role-based access and secure handling of credentials | Jeff | Open |
| R06 | Technical difficulties may delay implementation | Medium | High | High | Prioritize core functionality, identify technical dependencies early and maintain a simplified fallback approach | Omogbolaga | Open |
| R07 | Team members may have competing schedules or delayed contributions | Medium | Medium | Medium | Set clear responsibilities, deadlines and regular check-ins | Benardette | Open |
| R08 | Changes to project requirements may affect the planned solution | Medium | Medium | Medium | Document agreed requirements and assess changes before incorporating them | Benardette | Open |
| R09 | Limited testing time may prevent adequate validation of the matching process | Medium | High | High | Prepare test data early and prioritize matching and core user workflows | Eulleria / Omogbolaga | Open |
| R10 | Users may not understand or trust the recommended matches | Medium | Medium | Medium | Clearly explain match recommendations and allow users/admins to review or reject unsuitable matches | Benardette / Team | Open |
| R11 | Integration between technical components may not work as expected | Medium | Medium | Medium | Test components together before final review and resolve integration issues early | Omogbolaga / Jeff | Open |
| R12 | Project documentation may become inconsistent across team members | Medium | Medium | Medium | Use the agreed architecture and requirements as the reference for all project documents | Benardette | Open |
